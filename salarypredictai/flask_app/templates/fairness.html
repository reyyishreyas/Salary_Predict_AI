Fairness Strengths
  • Location Parity: Predictions show reasonable variance across locations, adjusted for cost of living
  • Department Balance: No extreme outliers detected in department-based predictions
  • Education Merit: Higher qualifications appropriately rewarded without over-weighting
  • Experience Curve: Smooth progression with years of experience
Areas to Monitor
  • Location Premiums: Tier-1 cities show 25-30% higher predictions - verify market rates
  • Role Concentration: Some niche roles have limited training samples
  • Career Gaps: Model may not fully account for career breaks or gaps
  • International Degrees: Premium for international degrees needs validation
Bias Mitigation Strategies

Continuously monitor predictions across different groups and conduct quarterly fairness audits to detect and address emerging biases early.

Ensure training data represents diverse candidates across locations, departments, backgrounds, and career paths to minimize systemic biases.

Regularly review feature importance and remove or adjust features that may introduce unintended bias while maintaining prediction accuracy.

Implement human review for predictions that deviate significantly from expected ranges or show unusual patterns across demographic groups.
