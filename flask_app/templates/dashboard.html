Welcome to Your Salary Prediction Dashboard

This is a comprehensive analytics platform designed to provide accurate salary predictions, monitor model performance, and ensure fairness — all in one intuitive interface.

Key Features:

  • Single Prediction: Input candidate details to get precise salary predictions with confidence ranges and top contributing factors.
  • Batch Prediction: Upload CSV files to process multiple candidates at once and download comprehensive results effortlessly.
  • Analytics & Insights: Visualize salary distributions, department trends, and experience vs. salary patterns for actionable decision-making.
  • Fairness Check: Identify potential biases across locations, departments, and education levels to ensure equitable predictions.
  • Model Health: Track total applicants processed, average predicted salary, and model status at a glance.
How to Use:
Utilize the Quick Actions section for fast access to predictions, analytics, and fairness checks. View charts and statistics to make informed, data-driven decisions.

Benefits:
Empower HR and recruitment teams to make transparent, reliable, and efficient salary predictions for individual candidates or entire organizations.
